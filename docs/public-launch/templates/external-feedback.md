# Feedback received outside GitHub

Use this only when someone gave feedback somewhere else (a comment, a
message, a conversation), would rather not open an issue themselves, and
**agreed** that you may record it ([feedback process](../feedback-process.md#where-feedback-comes-in)).
Open an issue with the matching label, paste the text below and fill it in.

Leave out anything they did not agree to share. Never paste a private
message verbatim without asking, and never include their name, employer or
contact details unless they asked for that.

---

**Feedback recorded by the maintainer on the reporter's behalf**

- **Received:** YYYY-MM-DD, via (channel type only, for example "LinkedIn
  comment" or "direct message")
- **Attribution agreed:** named as @handle / anonymous
- **Quoting agreed:** yes / no (if no, the summary below is paraphrased)

**What they tried:** version, platform, provider, as far as known. Write
"unknown" for anything they did not say; do not fill gaps with guesses.

**What happened:** their account, quoted if agreed, otherwise summarized.

**Their assessment:** what worked, what did not, what they expected.

**Follow-up:** whether they are willing to answer questions, and where.
