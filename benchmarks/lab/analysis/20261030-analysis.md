*Classified failures* are failed runs a pattern explains by definition (its own tests passed and the hidden ones failed, say). The failure rates with and without a pattern use its other kinds only, and are an association over these runs, not a cause. A pattern is *recurring* in two or more tasks and three or more runs, and *associated* when the runs with it fail more often beyond chance (90% intervals apart).
